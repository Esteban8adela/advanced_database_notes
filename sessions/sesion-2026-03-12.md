## Topics covered
- Operators: MINUS, UNION, MINUS ALL, UNION ALL, INTERSECT

## What I understood
- MINUS: Takes the results of the first query and subtracts any rows that also appear in the second query. Only returns unique rows

- INTERSECT: It returns only the unique rows that exist in both the first and second queries.

- UNION: Combines the results of two queries into a single list and removes any duplicate rows.

- ALL: removes the 'distinct' property of union and intersect
