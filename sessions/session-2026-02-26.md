## Topics covered
- Aggregate functions (COUNT, MIN, MAX, AVG, SUM)

- Grouping data with the GROUP BY clause

- Filtering grouped aggregates using the HAVING clause

## What I understood
- Aggregate functions: They take multiple rows of data and summarize them into a single value.

- GROUP BY: This clause allows me to split data into distinct buckets and run aggregate functions on each bucket independently.

- WHERE vs. HAVING: The WHERE clause filters individual rows before they are grouped and aggregated. The HAVING clause filters the aggregated groups after they have been created.
